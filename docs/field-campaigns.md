# Field Campaigns

Steve Nesbitt has participated in 27 major field campaigns spanning 1998–2025, spanning roles from radiosonde scientist and aircraft scientist to principal investigator of large multi-agency international deployments.

---

## Recent Campaigns (2018–2025)

### RELAMPAGO-CACTI (2018–2019)
**Remote sensing of Electrification, Lightning, And Mesoscale/microscale Processes with Adaptive Ground Observations — Cloud, Aerosol, and Complex Terrain Interactions**  
*Córdoba, Argentina*

Lead Principal Investigator of a $25M+ joint NSF/DOE/NASA/NOAA initiative deploying over 100 scientists across 7 agencies in Argentina. RELAMPAGO-CACTI studied extreme convective storms, orographic convection, and cloud microphysics in the lee of the Andes.

### CROCUS — Urban Climate Initiatives (2024–2025)
**Community Research on Climate and Urban Science**  
*Chicago, IL*

Principal Investigator. DOE-funded study of urban effects on precipitation, flooding, and the local water cycle in the greater Chicago metropolitan area.

### Lake Effect Snow Microphysics (2022–2024)
*Buffalo, NY*

Study of microphysical processes in lake-effect snowstorms using polarimetric radar and in situ aircraft observations.

### PERiLS (2022–2023)
**Propagation, Evolution, and Rotation in Linear Storms**  
*Southeastern United States*

Collaborative NSF/NOAA field campaign examining the mechanisms of tornado production and severe weather in quasi-linear convective systems.

## Selected Mid-Career Campaigns (2008–2016)

### OLYMPEX (2015)
**Olympic Mountain Experiment**  
*Pacific Northwest, USA*

NASA Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) ground validation campaign examining orographic precipitation in the Olympic Mountains.

### GCPEX (2011–2012)
**Global Precipitation Measurement Cold-season Precipitation Experiment**  
*Ontario, Canada*

NASA GPM ground validation of snowfall retrieval algorithms.

### CAMP²Ex (2019)
**Cloud, Aerosol and Monsoon Processes Philippines Experiment**  
*Philippines*

NASA field campaign studying aerosol-cloud-precipitation interactions in the maritime tropical environment.

### NSF OPEL-WG (2008–2011)
**Orographic Precipitation and Extremes over the Western Ghats**  
*Karnataka, India*

Principal Investigator. Study of extreme monsoon rainfall along the windward slopes of the Western Ghats.
